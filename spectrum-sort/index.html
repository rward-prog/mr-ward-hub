SpectrumSort

Murphy dropped the light waves. Put them back in order, fast and neat.

Pick a mode

Easy

Five rounds. Each round gives you one rule, like “sort by wavelength, longest to shortest.” Score goes on the Easy leaderboard.

Challenge

Six rounds. The rule is a surprise every time. You get a 3-second countdown to read it, then the clock starts.

Adventure

Five missions. Help Murphy pick the best light to see through a wall, send a signal, clean a bowl, study an exploding star, and fix a sunburn. Earn stars and a badge.

How to move the cards

  • Drag one card and drop it on another card. They swap places.
  • Or tap one card, then tap a second card to swap them.
  • The round ends the moment your order is right. No “check” button.

How points work (Easy and Challenge)

StartEvery round starts at 1,000 points.
TimeYou get 10 free seconds. After that you lose 6 points every second.
SwapsEach round has a “par,” the fewest swaps possible. Every swap over par costs 20 points.
Murphy’s hintOne hint per round. It costs 150 points.
On parFinish exactly on par and earn +100.
Fast is good. Neat is better. Random swapping costs you points.

In Adventure you earn up to 3 stars per mission. A clean mission is 3 stars, one slip is 2 stars, and more slips is 1 star. Finish all five to earn a badge.

The leaderboard and your three letters

  • After a full run you can type three letters to post your score. Letters only, A to Z.
  • Please do not use your name. Pick something fun, like MUR for Murphy.
  • Rude letter combinations get rejected, so pick something kind.
  • Your own best score is saved on your device only.

Cheat sheet

Open it if you want a study boost

From radio to gamma, here is the order of the light family:

RadioMicrowaveInfraredVisibleUltravioletX-rayGamma

Memory trick: Raging Martians Invaded Venus Using X-ray Guns.

Going from radio to gamma:

  • Wavelength gets shorter.
  • Frequency gets higher.
  • Energy goes up.
  • The temperature of the thing that glows goes up.
  • Danger goes up.
Watch out: wavelength is the odd one out. It goes the opposite way. “Shortest wavelength first” starts at gamma, but “lowest energy first” starts at radio. Read each rule twice!

What you are practicing

  • Putting the electromagnetic spectrum in order by wavelength, frequency, and energy.
  • Linking the temperature of an object to the kind of light it gives off.
  • Picking the safest light for a job, and explaining why.
